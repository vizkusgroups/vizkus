01 / About

We show up before the whitepaper and stay past mainnet.

Vizkus Group started as a two-person events crew running side-stage activations at conferences no one had heard of yet. Nine years on, we run the full stack for Web3 teams — the room where your community meets you, the interface where they meet your product, and the code that keeps both honest.

Design and engineering sit in the same sprint.
No account layer between you and the person doing the work.
One studio for events, product, content and code.
